## Business Case Summary
The iShares Enhanced Strategic series represents a diversified, passively-managed solution built on BlackRock's iShares ETF platform. The series addresses demand for low-cost, transparent, and tax-efficient managed portfolios across the risk spectrum. Five portfolios—Conservative, Moderate, Balanced, Growth, and Aggressive—serve different investor risk profiles with Strategic Asset Allocation (SAA) ranges tailored to each investor segment. Each portfolio employs a risk-controlled approach to portfolio construction, starting with a strategic asset allocation suited to investor profile, then using researched investment exposures to implement that allocation efficiently. The process considers expected return, volatility, liquidity, diversification, cost and downside risk.

**Portfolio Investment Objectives:**
- **Conservative:** To match or outperform the Morningstar Australia Conservative Target Allocation benchmark over a rolling three-year period
- **Moderate:** To match or outperform the Morningstar Australia Moderate Target Allocation benchmark over a rolling five-year period
- **Balanced:** To match or outperform the Morningstar Australia Balanced Target Allocation benchmark over a rolling five-year period
- **Growth:** To match or outperform the Morningstar Australia Growth Target Allocation benchmark over a rolling five-year period
- **Aggressive:** To match or outperform the Morningstar Australia Aggressive Target Allocation benchmark over a rolling five-year period

## Investment Process
**Investment Approach Summary:** BlackRock Investment Management (Australia) Limited employs a disciplined, diversified approach to portfolio construction for the iShares Enhanced Strategic series. Each portfolio begins with a strategic asset allocation suited to its investor risk profile, then uses BlackRock's iShares ETF suite to implement that allocation efficiently. The manager uses research to support asset allocation decisions while maintaining focus on expected return, volatility, liquidity, diversification, cost and downside risk management. Balanced portfolios place greater emphasis on spreading risk across growth and defensive assets, while high-risk portfolios allocate more heavily to equities and other growth-oriented investments. Portfolios are reviewed regularly, with rebalancing used to keep risk exposures aligned with objectives. Active trading is used to manage exposures, with expected turnover of 25-40% per annum.
