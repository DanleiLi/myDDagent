## 3. INVESTMENT STRATEGY AND ASSET ALLOCATION

### 3.1 Series Design and Objectives

The Aurora series comprises two complementary portfolios offering a simplified 0/100 growth-to-defensive split:

**Aurora Growth Managed Portfolio (NTH0620)** is designed for clients with a 10-year investment horizon and moderate-to-high risk tolerance. It targets a return in excess of CPI + 3.75% p.a., net of indirect fees, over a rolling 10-year period. The portfolio employs a 98% growth / 2% defensive (cash) allocation.

**Aurora Defensive Managed Portfolio (NTH0619)** is designed for clients with a 2-year investment horizon and low-to-medium risk tolerance. It targets a return in excess of CPI + 0.5% p.a., net of indirect fees, over a rolling 2-year period. The portfolio employs a 100% defensive (fixed interest and cash) allocation.

Both portfolios employ a strategic asset allocation (SAA) framework reviewed regularly by Salita's investment committee. The SAA is implemented using a blend of active and passive strategies, with active management concentrated in areas where the manager has high conviction.
