## Investment Process

The iShares Enhanced Strategic Portfolios employ a disciplined, diversified investment approach centered on strategic asset allocation, risk-controlled implementation and regular rebalancing. [IMQuestionnaire_IM Info.md](/wiki/IMQuestionnaire_IM_Info.md)

**Investment Philosophy:**

BlackRock's approach to these managed portfolios reflects a commitment to risk-controlled, diversified investing aligned to investor objectives and time horizons. The manager uses a strategic asset allocation framework suited to each portfolio's investor profile, with allocations determined by expected return, volatility, liquidity, diversification, cost and downside risk considerations. Conservative portfolios emphasize spreading risk across growth and defensive assets with bias toward capital preservation. Growth-oriented portfolios allocate more heavily to equities and alternative exposures, while maintaining diversification to manage drawdown risk.

**Implementation:**

The portfolios are implemented through a carefully curated selection of BlackRock iShares exchange-traded funds, offering institutional-quality diversification with transparent, low-cost structures. Underlying holdings span:
- Australian equities (S&P/ASX 200 large cap, diversified equity exposures)
- International equities (developed and emerging markets, by region and style)
- Fixed income (Australian and global government and corporate bonds, inflation-linked bonds)
- Real assets (listed property and infrastructure, domestically and internationally)
- Alternative exposures (gold, commodities, as appropriate to portfolio risk profile)
- Cash and money market instruments (daily liquidity buffer, minimum 1.5%)

**Portfolio Monitoring and Rebalancing:**

Portfolios are monitored regularly to ensure ongoing alignment with stated risk targets (8.5–10.5% volatility band for all portfolios). Rebalancing is conducted on a periodic basis or when allocations drift beyond defined policy ranges, with the aim of maintaining strategic exposure while managing costs through selective trading. The investment process incorporates quarterly reviews, scenario and stress testing, and regular communication with the responsible entity and platform partners.
